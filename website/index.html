Public beta for macOS

Glissform

Subtle animations and quality-of-life improvements for everyday interactions on your Mac.

Download beta 0.1.0-beta.1

macOS 15 or later · Tested on MacBook Air M5 15-inch · Other Macs are unverified

Seamless Close

Your desktop appears to stay in place as you close your MacBook lid, with perspective, frost, and shadow following the movement. It needs Screen Recording permission for one temporary screenshot per gesture.

Battery Ripple

A short wave responds when power connects or the built-in battery reaches 20%. Each event has its own switch. It does not capture your screen.

Install the beta

  1. Open the DMG and drag Glissform into Applications.
  2. Open Glissform. This beta is not Apple notarized, so macOS may block its first launch. If you trust this download, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway after the blocked attempt. See Apple’s instructions.
  3. Grant Screen Recording access if you want to use Seamless Close. Future beta updates can be checked and installed from the app.
